Before You Exit — Please Read
Exiting a vehicle is a high-risk action. Take a moment to check.
Even when the door is unlocked, the surroundings can still be unsafe. Before stepping out, please:
  • Look for moving traffic on the side you're about to exit on — including bikes and scooters.
  • Confirm an on-site team member or operator has waved you out, if one is expected.
  • Hold the door, step out slowly, and watch your footing — curbs and uneven ground are common.
  • If anything feels uncertain, stay inside and call the operator.
